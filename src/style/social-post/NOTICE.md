# Social Post — adapted writing rules

The social-post composer adapts writing modes and rules from **claude-skill-social-post**
by Hao0321 contributors: https://github.com/Hao0321/claude-skill-social-post

Original license: MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Hao0321 contributors.
The MIT permission notice is reproduced in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` at the repository root.

What was adapted (summarised and rewritten in TypeScript prompt form):
- Writing modes A (daily/observation), B (result/ship), C (long post/retrospective) from `voice_quick.md`.
- The five-field voice card (core tone, sentence/paragraph length, typical opening, typical ending, banned patterns).
- Priority order "user's explicit instruction > safety & platform limits > voice card > formula".
- Evidence rules (numbers need a source, otherwise leave blank; never invent facts).
- Platform notes: no external links in Facebook/Threads body; Instagram needs an image or video.
- Publishing requires explicit confirmation — here strengthened to **draft only, never publish**.

Not adapted: the local workbench, outcome ledgers, analytics, Chrome/CUA comment automation and publishing.
